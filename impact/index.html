Impact

Water treatment needs better membranes – without PFAS and toxic solvents.

The industry needs a sustainable alternative that does not compromise performance.

The challenge

Today’s solution carries a hidden footprint.

Polymeric ultrafiltration is a powerful water-treatment tool, but conventional membrane production can require hazardous organic solvents and persistent fluorinated materials.

These choices affect chemical handling, worker protection, solvent management, and long-term regulatory exposure. Changing the manufacturing chemistry can address these burdens at their source.

Our direction

Sustainability without compromising membrane performance.

01

Remove toxic solvents

Use water as the process medium and reduce the need for solvent recovery and handling.

02

Avoid persistent materials

Build a membrane platform without PFAS and without BPA-derived membrane polymers.

03

Enable responsible scale

Develop around industrial formats so sustainability can move beyond the laboratory.

Evidence first

We separate ambition from proof.

Poroplex is in development. Environmental benefits, manufacturing savings, and application performance must be demonstrated under relevant industrial conditions.

Our pathway is to establish reproducibility, qualify materials and modules, test with real water, and quantify the impact with partners. We will communicate validated results as they become available.
