Technology

Our technology controls membrane structure using water, not solvents.

Water-based process for high-performance UF membranes.

The principle

Same performance. A cleaner process. A more sustainable industry.

Membrane performance depends on carefully controlling pore formation. Poroplex uses interactions between charged polymers to create that structure in an aqueous system.

The result is a development platform for porous hollow fibers without relying on PFAS, BPA-derived polymers, or toxic organic processing solvents. The process is currently moving through reproducibility, scale-up, and application validation.

Development status: Poroplex is an early-stage technology venture. Product specifications and commercial claims remain subject to industrial qualification.

Technology pathway

Patented technology. Scalable process. Versatile polymer platform.

01
