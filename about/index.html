About Poroplex

Membrane science with a mission.

Poroplex brings together membrane technology, process engineering, and business development to make water treatment materials safer and more sustainable.

Our origin

Research translated for real-world water treatment.

Poroplex grew from membrane research at RWTH Aachen University and a simple question: can high-performance membranes be made without the chemistry the industry is trying to leave behind?

Our team is developing that answer into an industrial technology. We combine scientific depth with hands-on experience in hollow fibers, real-water systems, scale-up, and technology transfer.
