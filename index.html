Why now

Water treatment needs better materials.

Membranes are essential to clean water, yet conventional polymer membrane production can depend on hazardous solvents and persistent fluorinated materials.

Regulation, workplace safety, and sustainability targets are changing what the membrane industry needs. Poroplex is translating membrane research into a cleaner manufacturing route—while keeping industrial requirements at the center.
